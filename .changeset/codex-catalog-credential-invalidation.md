The daemon's credential invalidation also drops the cached Codex model and effort catalog (#610). After a setup-job login, a profile created, toggled or removed, or a secret set or deleted, the next read of a Codex home asks `model/list` again, where it could previously answer with the earlier account's models and effort ladders for up to ten minutes. The drop runs before the configuration and quota reads that can fail. A probe already running when the catalog is dropped, by this invalidation or by harness maintenance, still answers the caller that started it, so that one call can carry the earlier account's rows or ladder into a listing or a run; it no longer stores its answer or its failure, so it cannot reach a later reader or replace what a newer probe stored. The same invalidation runs on every settings save, whatever it changed, and on any harness's login window, and it drops every home, so the next read of each home spawns one prompt-free `app-server` child again (concurrent reads of an empty entry each spawn their own, as on any miss). The cache belongs to the daemon process and nothing re-reads it on a schedule: after a change the daemon does not observe, such as a vendor login or logout it did not run, a live answer stays until its first read past ten minutes, an observed invalidation, a changed binary or a daemon restart. The account view retains its own copy past those ten minutes until an observed invalidation, harness maintenance, an Accounts refresh or a restart discards it, and a refresh inside them retains the adapter's cached rows again.
